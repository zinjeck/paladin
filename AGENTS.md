# Paladin art and rendering requirements

- Citizen and soldier art follows the user-approved compact overhead style in `handoffs/art-direction/characters-v1/STYLE.md`. Use its palette-constrained exports as scale and silhouette references; keep male/female equipment equivalent and distinguish variants with restrained hair details.

- Every world sprite must share the same final pixel size. The canonical grid is 16 art pixels per logical tile (`WorldPixelGrid.h`). City and world scenes must pass through `WorldPixelScene`, including terrain, fitted roofs, attachments, entities, animation and lighting. Never bypass this with a separately drawn high-resolution world sprite. HUD icons use native pixels without arbitrary rescaling.
- Before delivering rendering or artwork changes, build and pass the `PaladinArtCheck` target. It checks actual day/night output pixel blocks, sprite integration, and resource cutout contamination. Inspect close-up and normal-view screenshots as well; file dimensions and palette checks alone are insufficient.
- Use the approved palette in `config/art-palette.hex` for source sprites. Runtime lighting is separate. Day should be vivid and luminous with colored shadows; night cool and dark with distinct warm local lights. Reference direction: No Game No Life by day, Death Note by night.
- Tribal houses should look inhabited through details attached to their wall planes. Do not overlay free-standing pottery on the facade. Ground props must sit on the ground with convincing contact.
- Inspect each exported atlas object independently. Never assume equal atlas cells isolate the intended object: neighboring foliage previously leaked into the meat icon.
- Keep source art, generated material and review images under `handoffs/art-direction/`; runtime PNGs belong in `assets/sprites/`. Do not put art sources in `out/` or change the user's Krita originals. Build-packaged copies of assets are expected beside the executable.
- Preserve distance caching, bounded work and pause-controlled animation. Do not allocate new textures on every zoom step.
- Terrain tiles are storage units, not visible square boundaries. City and world coasts and material transitions must use shared natural contours at every zoom. Reduce detail when zooming out; never introduce square tile corners or blur remaining features.
- Grass remains textured everywhere. Vary cluster shapes, spacing and shadows continuously across tile boundaries; do not create featureless green areas as a substitute for variation.
- Build local C++ executables after changes. Work directly in the PC checkout; do not create GitHub PRs unless explicitly requested.

- Enclosed buildings reserve a full tile-wide wall ring. The usable interior is at least 3x3 (minimum outside blueprint 5x5); doors occupy non-corner wall tiles. Placement, navigation, beds, furniture, storage and rendering must agree on this interior. Adjustable outdoor work zones require at least 3x3; adjustable enclosed buildings require at least 5x5. Adjustable placement always begins with a single-tile hover preview; roads remain 1x1. The city keep has a 3x5 interior inside a 5x7 footprint.
- Walls use shared material families with consistent texture scale, detailed caps and visible depth. Thatched roofs fit the outer walls, have pitched faces and irregular small straw edges, and keep all roof variants interchangeable.
- All enclosed buildings using the shared adobe design have dimensional timber corner posts, connected frame beams and worn clay foundations. Interior cutaways use a continuous equal-height low wall ring on all four sides with no corner dividers; door leaves and exterior wall accessories are hidden.

- Cut wall tops share the outer plaster base color and retain depth shading. Adobe uses the former muted wood-floor brown; wood floors use a lighter, warm honey palette. Material substitutions must stay within config/art-palette.hex.

- City depth uses colored contact shading at wall joints and beneath raised objects; preserve source PNGs and cached foliage batching. Low interior walls are one quarter tile high, with dimensional outline shading and short floor contact shadows.
- City tree species are stable per tile: select exactly one species per existing tree site, never extra sites. Conifers increase continuously as world-derived temperature falls (none in equatorial warmth, about 11% at temperate .5, exclusively conifers at .34 and below); birch is 6% of remaining deciduous trees. Trees retain building-clearance bounds and the canonical art grid.

- The world clock is authoritative; city HUD and work/rest schedules use longitude-based local solar time. Region selection must not change the world clock. All globe/city sunlight uses PlanetAstronomy. The planet's axis is 23.5 degrees; orbital phase remains fixed at equinox until seasons are explicitly implemented. Settlement maps retain geographic U/V for future hemisphere-aware seasons.
- Profile continuous city zoom and fast scrolling in the actual Debug executable, not only warmed stationary Release views. Preserve source detail; avoid world-only per-pixel work in city terrain and prewarm only the adjacent zoom range. Rendering translation units stay optimized in Debug while application/simulation retain runtime debugging checks.

- World cartographic annotations (settlement symbols and names) are native-screen plates, not world sprites. Keep their compact screen dimensions out of the zoom-dependent 32-pixel world-object raster. Actual strategic geometry retains that lattice; both use the terrain source camera and shared final residual. Never reintroduce per-marker coarse-pitch rounding.
- World coastal political fill, civic ink and relief share `WorldPoliticalSurface.h` with terrain. Water cannot receive political ink. Terrain/influence/controller revisions invalidate that shared presentation; drawing never mutates either territorial model.
- `PaladinArtCheck` also runs `PaladinWorldSurfaceTests`: real coast masks, rolled/flat camera motion, map modes, and cached solar optics. Inspect the `pr25-*` review images and motion CSV. Cached solar light is a native-resolution runtime optical effect, not source sprite art; every term must taper before its texture boundary.

- PR29 world presentation replaces settlement sprawl and population-tier world art with universal 18-native-pixel city/building and fortress symbols. Their geometry never grows with population. Preserve real strategic roads, separate from removed decorative settlement sprawl.
- World Employment is Diplomacy; city Employment remains unchanged. Outer-zoom realm picking, selected border ink, government thematic fill and reported area use the same dry civic/tribal presentation query. Tribal influence must not become binary ownership. PR30 supersedes PR29 population coloring: use the geographic census described below, not realm-wide totals.
- Army world icons use the packaged militia walking PNG, the shared strategic pixel lattice and a cached alpha-silhouette selection contour. Never draw a selection box or highlight an unselected unit. Icons and picking share the 10-to-16-effective-pixel close-view visibility band. Marching takes five game minutes per tile. Military cards are compact 76x88 portraits in a vertically scrollable grid above all recruitment/organization controls.

## PR30 continuity contracts

- The PR30 stockpile-art pause was superseded by the user's 2026-09-22 correction request. Preserve the freely drawable footprint and real inventory contents. StockpileShelterLayout owns roof, eave, platform and support coordinates; convert explicit image bounds through sprite pivots/elevation rather than offsetting the roof independently.
- City selection borders share snapped edges and an integral scene-lattice stroke. Citizen selection comes from the exact rendered sprite/animation-frame alpha mask, including selected distant citizens, never a box or a tint inside the figure. An undersized single-tile placement preview must not reach a building recipe that expands it to the minimum footprint; this was the detached barracks carpet bug.
- Realm laws default to Absolute Monarchy, Blood citizenship and Male-Dominated gender rights. Alternative choices remain disabled. Gender eligibility is a shared military/future-appointment predicate. Laws show one universal Reforms pane; work-day changes reach every owned settlement and newly materialized local maps.
- Technology has Military, Administration, Commerce and Production trees. Blank branches are deliberately locked placeholders. Administration/Citizenship is the only implemented technology; researching it is immediate and free in this pass. Preserve mouse-cursor zoom, drag-pan, per-tab views and title-bar window dragging.
- A resident has a primary culture, optional distinct secondary culture, citizenship realm and birth settlement. Citizenship makes local culture primary while preserving an immigrant's previous culture as secondary. Nearby immigrant origins are at most eight AI settlements within 30 angular degrees; never silently generate an unrelated global culture or enable admission when no origins exist. Parents both sharing the dominant settlement culture give their child only that culture. Blood citizenship requires local birth and at least one citizen parent.
- Demobilization returns the same person to civilian population and removes their soldier employment. Preserve fractional demographic carry on exact +/- one-person transfers. Returning a civilian does not automatically rehire a reserve. Return destinations are the soldiers' recorded recruitment cities; missing records or an unusable return site prevent partial discharge. Do not teleport field armies during Citizenship synchronization.
- Population mode uses WorldPopulationField, not a uniform per-realm fill. Detailed residents are located from homes or known citizen/keep positions. Aggregate AI cities use an explicitly estimated, connected dry-land footprint; it is not simulated block-by-block urban sprawl. Preserve census mass, longitude wrapping, terrain relief, unclaimed geography and real roads in flat and globe views. Government mode remains terrain-blanked. Both legends and P/T/G/N controls belong directly above the bottom-right minimap.
- Settlement markers open a movable inspector. Foreign cities are read-only and must not change the owned active settlement or create an AI local map. Quantities, destinations and Stop controls belong to WorldSettlementPanel. Panel dragging, scrolling and text editing consume input before the camera, HUD or map. Do not restore the selected-army HUD covering top-left information; Escape, blank-map clicking and repeated icon clicking provide deselection.
- WorldShipmentSystem owns physical cargo: debit unreserved public source stock once, carry it, then credit destination storage or keep-side ground piles once. Own-settlement routes only, land-only cardinal navigation with longitude wrapping, ten game minutes per caravan tile, at most 64 active routes per realm. Sustained routes wait for goods and preserve cargo on stop/capture/blockage; never delete goods in transit. Ground-pile overflow applies to detailed local maps; strategic settlements keep aggregate resource ledgers until materialization.
- AiRealmSystem is bounded daily/weekly decision work, not a per-frame full-world planner. Strategic garrisons draw real eligible people and existing food/rations, respecting law, personality and civilian food floors. AI logistics uses the same conserved caravan system. AI-AI trade/alliance/gift decisions use pairwise opinions and a 45-degree settlement-to-settlement diplomatic range. No out-of-range action may mutate state. Gifts transfer existing treasury money and use size-aware suggestions.
- PR31 adds field-army pursuit/contact, personnel-conserving autoresolve, and a temporary battlefield deployment view with Retreat. Tactical combat, sieges, conquest, automatic invasion targeting, alliance military intervention, periodic tribute and cross-realm trade cargo remain future work. Negotiated player-offer acceptance is separate from existing immediate valid-action diplomacy.
- BattleScene owns only temporary terrain, renderer resources and presentation records referring to canonical SoldierIds; never register a battlefield as a settlement or duplicate citizens. Contact and Fight pause the shared clock; battlefield speed controls may resume the world, and Retreat/result Continue restore the pre-encounter pause/speed. Battle input owns the modal/scene before normal world or city controls. Destroy the temporary scene on exit while retaining the shared resident asset library.
- PR30 regression coverage includes society, shipment conservation, AI decisions, geographic census, actual application mouse/keyboard routing, 144 animated alpha-contour comparisons, and all existing world surface/solar/marker tests. Keep the 80 ms software-globe worst-frame check intact. Conservative off-screen mesh rejection must retain at least one scene-lattice cell of guard coverage and may not discard any visible terrain detail.

## City trade and relief corrections (2026-09-22)

- Player depot orders are coordinated in src/simulation/WorldMarketOrders.cpp. DepotCollection is the shared eligibility/remaining-batch policy for detailed and frozen hauling. Fund and route each sale before authorizing collection; all named resources use city-wide collection, never the optional stockpile collection radius. Storage-room routes prefer the real doorway. Import inventory is separate from export staging and remains paid local supply.
- City mountain topology comes from broad, warped landforms conditioned on source-world relief. Smaller hill forms and contour-following foothills are distinct. Open basins may join into natural passes, but no mandatory cross-map stripe, sine-wave corridor, or cave mask may slice the whole terrain. Caves start at existing accessible rock edges and stay bounded inside mountain masses. Do not restore the superseded almost-all-solid-map test that required uniform crossing corridors.
